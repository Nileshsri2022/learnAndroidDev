### **1. What You Accomplished Today**

Day 2 was the longest day so far — roughly **two hours of video**, plus exercises, so
considerably more effort than that on your side.

| ✅ Learned | Where it showed up |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Variables** — `val` vs `var` | Storing player and computer choices |
