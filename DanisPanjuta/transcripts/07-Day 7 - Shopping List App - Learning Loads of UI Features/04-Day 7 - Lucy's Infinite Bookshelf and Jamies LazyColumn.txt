So in the heart of a bustling city, there was a magical bookstore owned by Lucy. One day, a curious young coder named Jamie visited Lucy's store, amazed by the infinite bookshelf. Jamie exclaimed, this is just like the lazy column in Jetpack Compose for Android app development. It only renders the items visible on the screen as users scroll. New items are composed and off screen items are discarded, ensuring optimal performance. Lucy smiled, realizing that her infinite bookshelf and lazy column shared the same magic. Efficiently displaying an endless list without a hitch. So I hope you enjoyed this story.
