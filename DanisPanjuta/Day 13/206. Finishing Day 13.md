## What you built and learned

- **Scaffold** structure: `Scaffold(topBar, floatingActionButton) { padding -> LazyColumn }`.
- **AppBarView**: reusable `TopAppBar` with `title` + hoisted `onBackNavClicked` + `colorResource`.
- **HEX colors**: `#RRGGBB` → `0xFFRRGGBB`, `colors.xml` centralization, `ColorResource` vs `Color(...)`.
- **Conditional back icon**: `showBackIcon` hides the `ArrowBack` on `HomeScreen`.
- **FAB**: `FloatingActionButton(Icons.Default.Add)` → `navigate(AddScreen/0L)` (add) vs `/${wish.id}` (edit).
- **Wish + WishItem**: `data class Wish` and `Card` row with `clickable`.
- **DummyWish**: hard-coded list to prove the `LazyColumn`.
- **Navigation**: `Screen` sealed class, `NavHost` with `AddScreen/{id}` `LongType` argument, `Navigation()` hosted in `MainActivity`.
- **AddEditDetailView**: `Scaffold` + two `WishTextField`s (wrapped `OutlinedTextField`) + empty-guard button.
- **WishViewModel**: `title`/`description` state + `Flow<List<Wish>>` + `addWish`/`updateWish`/`deleteWish` (stubs for Room).
- **NavigateUp**: `navController.navigateUp()` from the bar's arrow.
