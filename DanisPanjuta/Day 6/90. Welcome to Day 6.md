## Why state matters

Compose does **not** let you poke individual views and change them by hand the way older XML code often did.

The pattern is:

1. The user does something (tap, type) → that is an **event**
2. You update a **state** variable
3. Compose **recomposes** (re-runs) the UI that depends on that state

Change the variable, and the screen follows. That is the whole point of today.
