# Challenge TextToSpeech

---

## Overview

Challenge lecture (no video transcript): integrate the TextToSpeech demo into the **7 Minute Workout** app yourself.

---

## Task

1. Make `ExerciseActivity` implement `TextToSpeech.OnInitListener`.
2. Initialise `tts` in `onCreate`, set the language in `onInit`.
3. When an exercise starts, **speak its name** (e.g. "Jumping Jacks").
4. Shut down the engine in `onDestroy`.

---

## Hints

- Reuse `speakOut(text)` from the demo.
- Call it in `setupExerciseView()` with `exerciseList!![currentExercisePosition].getName()`.
- Optionally announce the **upcoming** exercise during the rest view instead.
