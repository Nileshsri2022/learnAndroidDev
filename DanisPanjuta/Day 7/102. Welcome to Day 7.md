# Welcome to Day 7 — Shopping List App

---

## Overview

**Course:** Complete Android 14 & Kotlin Development Masterclass  
**Chapter:** Day 7 — Shopping List App — Learning Loads of UI Features  
**Lecture:** Welcome to Day 7

Last day of week 1. You will build a **shopping list** you can actually use: add items, edit them, delete them.

---

## What you will learn today

* More UI interaction (buttons, dialogs, list rows)
* **State** again — this time a **list** of objects, not a few strings
* New Kotlin: lambdas, `map`, `copy`, `let`
* New Compose: `LazyColumn`, `AlertDialog`, `IconButton`, `BasicTextField`
* Splitting UI into **your own composables** (`ShoppingListApp`, `ShoppingListItem`, `ShoppingItemEditor`)

You will repeat yesterday’s ideas (state, `remember`, events) in a bigger layout.

---

## What you will not learn today

**Persistent storage.** Close the app and the list is gone. Room database comes later (around days 10–14). Today is UI + in-memory list.
