### **3. Why Lists Are the Missing Piece**

The Day 3 coffee machine could only handle **one order at a time**. There was no way to hold
several coffees, several bank transactions, or several of anything. Lists fix that — and the
same idea powers every real app: a shopping list, a chat history, a list of recipes from an
API.
