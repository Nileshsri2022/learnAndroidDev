### **What it is**

A PDF/reference sheet attached to the lecture, summarising Day 4's syntax so you can check
how something is written without scrubbing back through the videos.

### **What it covers**

The same ground as the [Day 4 summary](74-summary-of-day-4.md):

* **Creating lists** — `listOf` (immutable) vs `mutableListOf` (mutable)
* **Indexes** — zero-based, and how they shift after add/remove
* **List methods** — `add`, `remove`, `removeAt`, `removeFirst`, `removeLast`, `set`,
  `contains`, `.size`
* **Reading and replacing** — `list[i]` and `list[i] = value`
* **`for` loops** — by value, by index, and `break`
* **`until` vs `..`** — exclusive vs inclusive ranges
* **Comments** — `//` and `/* … */`
* **Encapsulation** — `private` properties with public member functions
