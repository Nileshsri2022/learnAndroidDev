# Adding A Little Border Around The Canvas

---

## Overview

Small design improvements: a grey **border** around the white drawing area (only the white area is drawable), and restoring the default action bar.

---

## Border drawable — `drawable/background_drawing_view_layout.xml`

```xml
<shape xmlns:android="http://schemas.android.com/apk/res/android"
    android:shape="rectangle">
    <solid android:color="@android:color/white" />
    <stroke android:width="1dp" android:color="#9AA2AF" />
</shape>
```

---

## Applying it

```xml
<eu.tutorials.kidsdrawingapp.DrawingView
    android:id="@+id/drawing_view"
    android:layout_width="0dp"
    android:layout_height="0dp"
    android:layout_margin="5dp"
    android:background="@drawable/background_drawing_view_layout"
    app:layout_constraintTop_toTopOf="parent"
    app:layout_constraintBottom_toTopOf="@id/ll_paint_colors"
    app:layout_constraintStart_toStartOf="parent"
    app:layout_constraintEnd_toEndOf="parent" />
```

- `layout_margin="5dp"` → grey root background visible around the canvas.
- Parent `ConstraintLayout` gets a light grey background so the frame stands out.

---

## Recap / cheat sheet

- **Frame:** shape drawable with `solid` + `stroke`, plus a margin.
- `0dp` + constraints = "match constraints" in ConstraintLayout.
