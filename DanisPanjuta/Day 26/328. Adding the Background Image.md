# Adding the Background Image

---

## Overview

Places an **ImageView behind the DrawingView** using a **`FrameLayout`** so the user can draw on top of a picture. The drawing view's background becomes transparent.

---

## FrameLayout = layers

A `FrameLayout` stacks its children **on top of each other** — later children are drawn above earlier ones.

```xml
<FrameLayout
    android:id="@+id/fl_drawing_view_container"
    android:layout_width="0dp"
    android:layout_height="0dp"
    android:layout_margin="5dp"
    android:background="@drawable/background_drawing_view_layout"
    android:padding="1dp"
    app:layout_constraintTop_toTopOf="parent"
    app:layout_constraintBottom_toTopOf="@id/ll_paint_colors"
    app:layout_constraintStart_toStartOf="parent"
    app:layout_constraintEnd_toEndOf="parent">

    <ImageView
        android:id="@+id/iv_background"
        android:layout_width="match_parent"
        android:layout_height="match_parent"
        android:scaleType="centerCrop"
        android:src="@drawable/image" />

    <eu.tutorials.kidsdrawingapp.DrawingView
        android:id="@+id/drawing_view"
        android:layout_width="match_parent"
        android:layout_height="match_parent"
        android:background="#80FFFFFF" />
</FrameLayout>
```

- Border and constraints move from the DrawingView to the **FrameLayout**.
- `#80FFFFFF` = white at 50 % alpha → the image shows through. (Alpha is the first byte: `#AARRGGBB`.)
- `scaleType="centerCrop"` fills the area without distortion.

---

## Recap / cheat sheet

- **Layers:** `FrameLayout` → `ImageView` (bottom) + `DrawingView` (top).
- **Transparent colours:** `#AARRGGBB`.
