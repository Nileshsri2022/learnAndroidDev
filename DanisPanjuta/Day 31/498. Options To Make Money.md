# Options To Make Money

---

## Overview

A resource lecture (no video transcript) on ways to earn money with Android skills.

---

## Options

| Path | Notes |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Job as Android developer** | Build a portfolio (GitHub + Play Store apps) |
| **Freelancing** | Upwork, Fiverr, Toptal, local businesses |
| **Your own apps** | Monetise via ads, in-app purchases, subscriptions, paid apps |
| **Contract work / agencies** | Short-term projects, often well paid |
| **Teaching / content** | Blog, YouTube, courses |

---

## App monetisation models

- **Ads** — Google AdMob (banner, interstitial, rewarded).
- **In-app purchases / subscriptions** — Google Play Billing Library.
- **Paid app** — one-time price (harder to gain users).
- **Freemium** — free core, paid premium features.

---

## Tips

- Solve a real problem for a specific audience.
- Portfolio quality > quantity; show clean, well-structured code.
- Keep learning — the ecosystem moves fast.

## Recap / cheat sheet

- Jobs, freelancing, own apps (AdMob / Play Billing), contracts, teaching.
